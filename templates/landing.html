Purpose-Built Architecture

Designed for Nigerian School Realities

Built from the ground up according to strict Nigerian school administrative requirements.

Strict Multi-Tenancy

Every school has a unique tenant identity. URL manipulation, parent data, and student records are strictly isolated at database query level.

Compulsory 40/60 Assessment

Enforces Nigeria's standard 40 marks Continuous Assessment (CA) and 60 marks Examination allocation with custom sub-components and report card printing.

Termly Fee Billing (₦)

Create termly fee schedules, apply individual student scholarships or discounts, allow parents to upload teller/transfer evidence, and empower bursars to verify.

Smart Attendance & Verification

Daily student attendance with 8:30 AM Africa/Lagos reminders. Teacher self check-in with immutable timestamps, awaiting principal verification.

Academic Calendar & Versioning

Central Nigerian public holidays combined with school-specific terms. Upload official calendar documents (PDF, DOCX) with revision tracking.

PWA & Offline Capability

Installable on Android and iOS home screens as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Caches essential assets and works reliably on low-bandwidth networks.

Role-Based Experience

Dedicated Portals for Every Stakeholder

A clutter-free, role-customized dashboard for teachers, bursars, parents, and principals.

Proprietor & Admin

Manage school registration, tuition pricing, staff permissions, high-level financial reports, and school operations.

Principal & Head Teacher

Check-in daily, record class roll calls, enter continuous assessment scores, submit terminal grades, and view assigned class timetables.

Compulsory Grading Policy

Strict Continuous Assessment (40) & Examination (60)

Individual schools cannot tamper with the compulsory 40/60 mark split, guaranteeing academic integrity across the board. Schools can however customize the components that total 40 marks (e.g., Test 1 = 15, Assignment = 10, Project = 10, Attendance = 5).

  • Result Approval Workflow: Teacher enters scores → submits for approval → Principal approves → Results published and locked.
  • Automated Report Cards: Downloadable terminal report cards complete with teacher remarks, principal remarks, attendance stats, and grade scale.
